SAY:
"A Job represents the lifetime and cancellation state of coroutine work.
Launch returns that handle, and cancel requests cancellation. A Job is not a
dedicated thread. Cancellation is cooperative, which is why delay responds
to it. Canceling a parent Job also cancels its child jobs.

This experiment showed me that starting loading and finishing loading are
different moments. It also showed why UI work needs lifecycle management.
Without the manual cancellation, work can continue while the screen is stopped.

RepeatOnLifecycle still uses coroutines and Jobs internally. It replaces my
manual field and callback bookkeeping with automatic cancellation and relaunch
at the chosen state. That reduces the chance of forgetting cancellation or
starting competing lifecycle-controlled loads. I restored that final version,
and the app displays the crimes after loading."
